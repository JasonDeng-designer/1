MDes Fashion & Textiles Glasgow School of Art Forced Amalgamation

This project explores forced amalgamation through material experimentation, inspired by the transformative logic of Annihilation. Fabrics are twisted, suspended, and displaced to push them beyond their natural state, revealing tension, instability, and resistance. Fragments of varied scale, texture, and origin are forcibly stitched together — intervention and repair. Colour adds a second disruption, using unstable, refracted tones applied through layering and seepage to suggest mutation. Colour and structure deliberately clash, creating ongoing imbalance. Rooted in body positivity, the work promotes size inclusivity, proposing that diverse bodies do not simply wear garments, but actively shape and redefine their form and meaning.

本项目通过材料实验探索强迫融合的概念，灵感来源于电影《湮灭》中的变异逻辑。作品通过扭转、悬置与位移，使织物脱离其自然形态，呈现张力、不稳定性与抵抗感。色彩作为第二重扰动，以层叠与渗透暗示突变；色彩与结构刻意冲突，形成持续的不平衡。作品植根于身体积极性与尺码包容，主张多样身体并非被动承载服装，而是主动塑造并重新定义其形态与意义。
