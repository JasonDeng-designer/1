Integration / New Human

整合 / 新人类

Fashion & Textiles Symbiosis Digital Life

The body as super-organism: microorganisms outnumber human cells; equilibrium is design. Integration proposes symbiosis as a fashion manifesto — microbial medicine, post-digital ontology, and a timeline from 2024 to 2055 self-portrait. The New Human is an advanced fusion of humans and microbiota: consciousness entering the cloud as a new form of life, rendered as a 3D character hero.

身体即超有机体：微生物数量远超人体细胞，平衡即设计。《整合》以共生为时装宣言——微生物医学、后数字本体论，以及 2024 至 2055 自画像时间线。《新人类》是人与菌群的进阶融合：意识进入云端成为新生命形态，并以三维角色英雄渲染呈现。
