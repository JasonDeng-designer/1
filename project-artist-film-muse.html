Artist Film Muse

艺术家电影缪斯

Fashion & Textiles Holy Mountain Bourdin / Bowie

Three starting points — Jodorowsky's The Holy Mountain, Guy Bourdin, and David Bowie — open a collage practice that becomes boards, then polyhedron cutting experiments. Silhouette structures extracted from those cuts combine prior outputs into final looks: fashion as a conversation between film, photography, and glam mythology.

三个起点——霍多罗夫斯基《圣山》、盖伊·布尔丹与大卫·鲍伊——开启拼贴实践，继而进入图板与多面体切割实验。自切割中提取的廓形结构，将前期产出合成为最终造型：时装作为电影、摄影与华丽神话之间的对话。
