Error Matrix

错误矩阵

Fashion & Textiles Cognitive Bias Information Overload

In an era of information overload, vast volumes of stimuli challenge attention, decision-making, and emotional stability. This project treats the brain's progressive transmission of information as a design system — extracting neuron morphologies, simulating glial connections with silicone and rubber bands, and translating cognitive bias into folding structures. Contours from painting experiments become panels in CLO and on the stand; colour and material sample the noise between signal and memory.

在信息过载的时代，海量刺激挑战注意力、决策与情绪稳定。本项目将大脑信息的渐进式传递视为设计系统——提取神经元形态，以硅胶与橡皮筋模拟胶质连接，并将认知偏差转译为折叠结构。绘画实验所得轮廓进入 CLO 与人台；色彩与材料取样信号与记忆之间的噪声。
